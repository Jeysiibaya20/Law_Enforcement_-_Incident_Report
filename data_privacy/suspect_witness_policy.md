# Suspect & Witness Data Privacy

## Purpose
This document explains how suspect and witness information is handled with the highest degree of privacy in Alertara PH.

## What data is protected
- Suspect name and alias
- Witness name and contact details
- Statement content
- Interview notes
- Photo attachments and identification documents
- Location and incident context

## Access control
- Only authorized admin users may access suspect and witness records.
- Sensitive suspect/witness records are restricted by case and role.
- Records are only shown in the context of case management and are not openly searchable across unrelated cases.

## Data handling rules
- Sensitive data is stored in the database with limited access.
- Uploaded files related to suspects and witnesses are saved in protected upload folders.
- Photo and ID attachments are only visible to users with access to the case.

## Sharing rules
- Information may be shared only with authorized law enforcement personnel and system administrators.
- Data cannot be exported or transmitted without approval.
- Witness protection indicators and privacy flags are respected at all times.

## Retention and deletion
- Suspect and witness records are retained according to case requirements.
- Deleted suspects are soft-deleted and can only be restored by an admin.
- User requests for deletion or correction are handled through official support channels.

## Notes
- If a record is flagged as witness protection, extra care is taken to hide identifying information.
- This document is intended as a high-level privacy overview and does not replace formal legal privacy policies.
